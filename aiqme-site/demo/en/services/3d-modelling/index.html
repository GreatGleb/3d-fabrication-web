3D modelling & reverse engineering

From a sketch or worn part to a production-ready model.

WHEN IT HELPS

The solution starts
with your need.

We create precise 3D models from drawings, photographs or scan data. From simple parts to complex assemblies, we prepare solutions for your specific needs.

  • Reconstruct damaged, worn or discontinued parts.
  • Adjust the geometry and construction of existing parts.
  • Adapt the model to the selected manufacturing process.
Start an enquiry
01 / INPUT

What should you provide?

A sketch, drawing, photos, scan data or the physical part. Explain key dimensions, loads and connections.

02 / OUTPUT

What will you receive?

An approved 3D model and agreed file formats. Drawings, a prototype and a manufacturing plan can be included.

A clear workflow.

01

Your challenge

Send a file, sketch, photo or the part itself. We will advise on a suitable way to make it and agree the price and timing.

02

We will scan or measure the part and prepare a digital file for manufacturing.

03

Before production, a team member checks the model and settings. We coordinate the agreed work.

04

We check the result and prepare it for delivery or collection.
